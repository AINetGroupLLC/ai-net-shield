# AI Net Shield

AI Net Shield is completely free, local-first, customer-operated readiness and security-assessment workbench software developed by AI Net Group LLC through its Research & Development Division and AI Net Labs.

It combines deterministic readiness scoring from self-reported answers, bounded point-in-time observations, supported third-party report imports, narrowly scoped authorized security workflows, limited human-approved managed-file operations, and optional customer-configured AI assistance. It complements existing protections; it is not an antivirus, firewall, or a certification of security or legal compliance.
